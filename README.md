# Telecom-Customer-Churn-
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Telecom Customer Churn using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to uncover key drivers of customer attrition and provide actionable retention strategies.

📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by telecom companies. This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a telecom customer dataset to identify the major factors influencing customer attrition and provide data-driven recommendations for improving customer retention.

The analysis focuses on customer demographics, service usage, contract types, billing information, and payment methods to understand churn behavior and uncover business opportunities.

🎯 Business Problem

Telecom companies lose significant revenue when customers discontinue their services. The objective of this analysis is to:

Understand why customers churn.
Identify high-risk customer segments.
Discover patterns influencing customer retention.
Provide actionable recommendations to reduce churn.

📈 Key Insights
1️⃣ Gender Is Not a Significant Churn Driver
Male and female customers exhibit similar churn behavior.
Gender-based retention strategies are unlikely to provide meaningful impact.
2️⃣ Senior Citizens Have Higher Churn Rates
Senior customers are more likely to discontinue services compared to non-senior customers.
Improved support and simplified plans can enhance retention.
3️⃣ Early Tenure Customers Are High-Risk
Customers with shorter tenure show significantly higher churn rates.
Long-term customers are considerably more loyal.
Business Recommendation

Focus on customer onboarding and engagement during the first 90 days.

4️⃣ Contract Type Strongly Influences Churn
High Churn
Month-to-Month Contracts
Low Churn
One-Year Contracts
Two-Year Contracts
Business Recommendation

Encourage customers to upgrade to longer-term contracts through incentives and bundled offers.

5️⃣ Value-Added Services Increase Customer Retention

Customers using:

Online Security
Device Protection
Tech Support

show lower churn rates compared to customers without these services.

6️⃣ Payment Method Impacts Churn

Customers paying through Electronic Check exhibit the highest churn tendency, whereas automatic payment methods are associated with better retention.

7️⃣ Monthly Charges vs Total Charges
Churned customers generally have higher monthly charges.
Retained customers accumulate higher total charges due to longer tenure.
